## Who uses Umucyo

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Cooperative administrators**

    ---

    Manage collection points, agents, devices, prices and tasks, and pay farmers every month.

-   **Collection agents**

    ---

    Register farmers, weigh and record tea deliveries, and complete assigned tasks.

-   **Farmers**

    ---

    Receive delivery confirmations by SMS and check their reports by USSD from any basic phone.

</div>
